About us

Built on deep expertise in data standards

Tool80 is a SME based in Trento (North Italy) carrying out research and development in the field of coding and decoding of multimedia data streams according to MPEG and MPAI standards.

We also bring expertise in generic data streaming across domains including open data, energy data and genomic data — providing consulting, design, and system integration support across a variety of platforms.
