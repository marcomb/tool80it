About us

Tool80 is a SME located in Trento (North Italy) that carries out research activities in the field of coding and decoding of multimedia data streams according to MPEG and MPAI standards.

We also have expertise in generic data streaming, such as open data, energy data and genomic data, and provide consulting, design experience and system integration support on a variety of different platforms and AI algorithms.
